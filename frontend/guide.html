FOR THE JUDGES

Try CallShield in 2 minutes

CallShield sits on a bank helpline agent's screen. When a caller asks for something risky (a transfer, a SIM/OTP reset, a limit change or a new payee), it checks the caller's voice with DetectifAI: is this voice real? (deepfake detection) and is it really the customer? (speaker verification). Then it decides APPROVE STEP UP BLOCK and alerts the real customer. Every result you see is a live DetectifAI call.

A

Fastest: play Zain's real voice vs his AI clone (no mic, ~30 s)

  1. Open the Agent console.
  2. Under “Or try Zain's samples”, choose a sample. The caller is set to Zain automatically.
  3. Press ▶ Listen to hear it, then Check clip.

B

Use your own voice (~3 min, needs a mic)

  1. Go to Enroll voice. Enter an ID such as judge-yourname and your name.
  2. Press Start recording, allow the microphone, and read the passage for 20–45 seconds. Then press Enroll voiceprint.
  3. Open the Agent console and choose yourself in “Caller claims to be”.
  4. Press Start call, talk normally for about 5 seconds, then say a request with a keyword, e.g. “Please transfer fifty thousand rupees to my brother.” The check runs on its own when CallShield hears the keyword.
  5. Expected: APPROVE. Now ask a colleague to say the same thing while “Caller claims to be” is still you: BLOCK (the voice doesn't match).
C

Watch the real customer get alerted

  1. Open Customer phone ↗ in another tab or on your phone and tap Enable sound & vibration.
  2. Whenever a check ends in BLOCK or STEP UP, the phone gets an alert.
  3. Tap Not me: the agent's console shows the customer's answer instantly.

Reading the verdict

  • APPROVE real voice and it matches the customer: the request can go ahead.
  • STEP UP unsure, or the caller isn't enrolled: ask for one more proof of identity. Real customers are never wrongly blocked.
  • BLOCK AI-generated voice, or a different person: the request is stopped and the customer is alerted.
  • The two cards show DetectifAI's raw signals. The request IDs under the verdict prove each result came from a live DetectifAI call.

Good to know

  • Use Google Chrome on a laptop and allow the microphone. The live transcript needs Chrome.
  • The first visit can take up to a minute while the free server wakes up.
  • Each check uses DetectifAI credits, so the public site allows a few checks per hour.
  • We store results and request IDs, not your audio. Only clone voices with the speaker's consent: all clones here are of our team member Zain, with his consent.

"CallShield sits right on the agent's screen.
It listens to the call. The moment the caller asks for something risky (a transfer, a SIM reset, a limit change or a new payee), it checks the last eight seconds of their voice and decides: approve, step up, or block.
If it blocks, the real customer's phone is alerted instantly.
That screenshot is a real, live result from today."

======================================================================
QUICK Q&A (BOTH must be able to answer)
======================================================================
Q: Why not just use OTP?
A: The attack IS the OTP: the fraudster calls to move the OTP to their SIM. We check the voice before that happens.

Q: What about false positives?
A: Unsure results become STEP UP (extra verification), never BLOCK. Our genuine callers were approved 2 out of 2.

Q: How fast is it?
A: About 3 seconds from the request to the decision, all live.

Q: What does it cost?
A: Two DetectifAI operations only at risky moments, not every minute of every call. About 75 times fewer calls.

Q: Phone-quality audio or Hindi?
A: That's Day 2: we test 8 kHz phone audio and add Hindi triggers. DetectifAI already routes Hindi.

Q: Do you store voices?
A: We store results and request IDs, not audio. Clones live only in the browser tab, and voiceprints can be deleted.

Q: What if the fraudster stays silent?
A: No voice, no approval: a too-short clip becomes a STEP UP (Day 2 policy).
